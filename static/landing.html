Turn YouTube videos into LinkedIn & Instagram posts

Nuvoletro automates the full repurposing pipeline: extract audio from YouTube, transcribe with OpenAI Whisper, retrieve platform playbooks via Chroma RAG, and generate promotional posts with the Gemini API — no manual copy-paste workflow.
