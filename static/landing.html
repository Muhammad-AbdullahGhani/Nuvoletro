Production Stack & Core Architecture

  • yt-dlp Engine High-speed automated YouTube audio & video stream extraction.
  • OpenAI Whisper Millisecond-accurate speech-to-text with word segment timestamping.
  • ChromaDB Vector Store Semantic RAG retrieval of high-converting platform copywriting playbooks.
  • Google Gemini 2.0 Flash Contextual, platform-tailored post generation with JSON schema validation.
  • FastAPI Worker Pool Parallel ThreadPoolExecutor job queues for high-throughput batch jobs.
  • FFmpeg 9:16 Video Slicer Automated vertical cropping (1080x1920) for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.
